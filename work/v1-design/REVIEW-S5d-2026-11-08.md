**C1. The S4 residue correction is added in the wrong frame on sys-chop runs.** PROVEN.
- **Where.** `model/studies/s4_corners.py`:
  - `_corr` (l.158–160), used by `static_err_corr` (l.163), `cm_err_v` (l.340) and `mc_task.cv` (l.245);

- **Problem.**
  - `iadc.convert` ends with `u_hat = u_hat * s`, but `x2_final` stays in the modulator frame. `_corr` adds `x2_final/(c1c2W)` without the `s`.
  - For s = −1 the error is 2·x2/(c1c2W), and the pair average keeps x2_minus/(c1c2W).
  - That makes the error ε-independent, ∝ 1/N², and present with an ideal OTA: exactly the WO-12 signature.
- **Reproduction** (tt/27/3.3, 2 MHz, ΔVcm 0.8 V; sys-on error in µV at ε 1e-4 / 4e-4 / 8e-4):

**M1. The R9 `cp_vg` hook models a permanent virtual-ground parasitic as a sampled cap.** PROVEN by algebra and by the deck's diagnostic rows.
- **Physics.** Charge conservation at vg = −vo/A:
  - Caps reset every cycle (Cs, Ck) contribute C·vg′.
  - A permanent Cp,vg contributes Cp·(vg′ − vg).
  - Result: vo′·(1 + (1+c+cp)/A) = vo·(1 + (1+cp)/A) + c·vin.
  - So Cp,vg lowers only g; the leak 1 − p ≈ c/A is unchanged. The hook gives (c+cp)/A, which overstates the leak.
- **Ck is correct as a sampled cap** (it is switched to CM while sampling): β2 = 1/(1 + c2(1+k)) = 0.571 against 0.667, and the leak rises by (1+k) = 1.5.
- **Evidence** (xcheck A60_base vs A60_base_cpvg):
  - Deck with Cp,vg 0.262 pF: INL 3.993 → 4.007 ppm.
  - Model with `cp_vg`: 3.090 → 5.278 ppm, ratio 0.76 (`cp_only`) / 0.60 (`cp_plus_ck`).
  - The deck is right.
- **Direction.** Split the hook in two:
  - a topology-derived sampled term `c_samp2 = c2·(1+k)` (D25 separate k-DAC), derived from k, not a stored ratio;
  - a permanent `cp_hold` that enters only g: p = (1 + (1+cp)/A)·g.

  Then recheck the A60_base_cpvg row; it should match within a few %.
