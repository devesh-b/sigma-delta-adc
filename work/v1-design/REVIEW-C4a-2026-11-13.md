# REVIEW-C4a-2026-11-13: switches / phase generator design note (WO-46)

Reviewer: sd-adc-design-reviewer. Read-only. Condensed by the coordinator.

## Verdict
**Revise the note, then netlist.** Sizes hold; no new spot run is needed.

**What held up:**
- TG Ron table and worst-corner choice;
- the 2/4 top-short rule;
- the D74 margin (~75 ns);
- spot discipline: 9/9 broken copies REJECT.

## Major
- **M1:** Order 1 has one extra loop delay compared with the model (DESIGN.md:62-67, iadc.py:9).
  - The comparator reads x2 = c2·x1 from the previous φS, so y[n+1] = sign(x1[n]); the model has y[n] = sign(x1[n]).
  - Order 2 matches the model.
  - Order-1 x1 swing, bitstream and golden vectors are therefore unchecked.
  - Coordinator note: a first-order loop with a full extra cycle of delay has NTF denominator 1 − z⁻¹ + z⁻², with poles on the unit circle. Its stability and swing must be checked in the model before accepting the delay.
- **M2:** Bottom-plate order margin is ~0.1–0.25 ns at ff/−40.
  - Early-to-late is 0.70 ns at the 50 % points, against a 0.57 ns fall and up to 0.24 ns T/C skew.
  - Fix: early fully off (10 %, T and C) before late starts. Lengthen K_E to 1–2 ns.
- **M3:** Injection tb #3 sweeps only the input CM.
  - The top-short charge splits by the signal-dependent input TG Ron. That error is odd in the input, so sys chop does not cancel it.
  - Fix: sweep the differential input and report INL in ppm.
- **M4:** Power-up: CL-5 cannot be met while VDPWR is missing, so a detector is required.
  - The TT PDK has `lsbuflv2hv_isosrchvaon_1` and `lsbuflv2hv_clkiso_hlkg_3` (SLEEP_B), and `lsbuflv2hv_symmetric_1` (PROVEN).
  - Ramp tb: no input-to-VREF crowbar, no 1.8 V gate overstress.

## Minor
- m1: Gated #1 TGs swap while φSl is high. This is harmless only if swap/short change inside the reset window: state that rule, or use the 3.3 V gap latch.
- m2: Measure the dead time g on the gated nets, including the cross terms (n gate of one phase vs p gate of the other).
- m3: The vg Ron ≤ 1 kΩ target is too tight. W 4–6 µm is enough.
  - The Sg leak depends on Vin_cm; tb #9 must sweep Vin_cm and record the sign.
  - A T-switch is an option.
- m4: The strobe φC needs an hv2lv shifter. It is missing from the C7 count.
- m5:
  - SPEC.md:298 still says CL-23 falling edge (now fixed by CL-28).
  - C3 DESIGN:41 has g = 0.05·T against SPEC's 0.025/fs.
- m6: S2cm (#7) belongs on φIl, Sk0 (#11) on φSl.
- m7:
  - DAC 4/12 is enough (6/18 optional).
  - The VREF− switch can be NMOS-only.
