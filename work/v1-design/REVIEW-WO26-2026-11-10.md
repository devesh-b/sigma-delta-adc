## Major
- **M1: HV MC in the sky130 PDK leaves out Vth mismatch (PROVEN).** `vth0_slope = 0` for both g5v0d10v5 devices in `parameters/invariant.spice`; mismatch comes only through toxe and nfactor. HV MC therefore under-reports offsets (WORKFLOW §11, fake MC). Cross-check against hand Pelgrom with A_VT about 8–12 mV·µm.
- **M2: three nominal margins are thinner than the expected mismatch (REASONED).**
  - **Boost SR:** 25.54 against 25 V/µs at sf/−40, a 2.2 % margin, against σ(I_tail ratio) of about 1.5–2 %. It does not survive 3σ. The 1.4 pF basis is a proxy (the real load gives about 36–42 V/µs); gate B with sr95 is the arbiter.
  - **V_OCM:** 30.8 against 38 mV, a 7.2 mV margin. m_bl0 is one finger, so σ ≈ 3–6 mV goes 1:1 into V_OCM, because the SC CMFB has no gain inside the loop.
  - **Hot span:** 1.414 V against the ±1.4 V table target; the signal needs about 1.2 V, so this is fine.
- **M3: the op/AC exit is permanently red** (`VERDICT REJECT (18)` from the ratio row alone). A gate that always fails cannot flag a new failure. After the item-1 decision, make the ratio row info-only, or bound it per corner by the derived rows.

## Item 1 (ratio exit) options
- (a) Accept the systematic offsets (CLM/DIBL with the Vds difference; deterministic and covered by PVT). The SR, ceiling, K_P-tracking and V_OCM rows are the real gates; give them a mismatch allotment. **Sound.**
- (b) Vds-matched cascoded references: optional. It mostly fixes temperature spread, which no dependent row needs.
- (c) Recentre I_REF_btp by +1.9 %: SR margin and ceiling become about 3.5 % / 3.5 %. This adds OTA current (D54, user).
