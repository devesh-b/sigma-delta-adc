## Major
1. **Precision A0 relies on the replica's own error (PROVEN).** `sizes_draft.py:100-101`, `netlist.py:120-122`.
   - The replica reads K_P +5–9 % high: the m_rn drain sits at about 2 V against about 0.5 V on m_n, and the subtraction multiplies that error by 1.59.
   - At cold, A0 moves about 2.5 dB per K_P unit. Without the error, fs/−40 would sit about 1.5 dB under 80.3.
   - Mismatch: 1-finger and 2-finger replicas give σ(K_P) ≈ 2.7 % and σ(A0) ≈ 0.7 dB (REASONED).
   - Direction:
     - KP_BANK base 5 → 4 (K_P 12.5, no extra current, base span ≥ 1.756 V);
     - cascode m_rn or match its drain voltage;
     - 2–4× more replica fingers.
2. **V_OCM error +58.8 mV (ff/125 boost) exceeds the SPEC row 98 / CL-2 bound of about 38 mV (PROVEN).**
   - The composite ratios drift in opposite directions over temperature: tail 1.15 → 1.25, sink 1.19 → 1.11, fixed load 1.09 → 1.15.
   - m_plc (a 25 % section) absorbs the I_NC error four times over. vbl0 is a minor term.
   - Direction: feed V_BL0 from a replica (I_NC replica − fixed-load replica), or use parallel-only unit-finger references.
3. **The I_tail ±3 % row stands in for the SR floor; widening it to ±5 % breaks SPEC row 95.**
   - At sf/−40 boost, 35.09 µA / 1.4 pF = 25.06 V/µs (floor 25). Base 17.73 µA gives 12.66 V/µs (floor 12.5).
   - Cost of ±4 % elsewhere: GBW −3 % (large margin), A0 +0.1–0.2 dB, noise +0.1 dB, power fine.
   - Sound row: SR ≥ floor at every corner and current ≤ ceiling, with the trim shifted about +2 % (about +1 µA hot).
   - Per-finger bulk ties: not sound. With a common bulk, series-parallel is exact, so the mechanism is likely DIBL/CLM at low per-finger Vds (REASONED). This corrects the WO-24 "body effect" wording.
   - A C6 reference with compensating drift: not sound, because it would cancel a model artifact.
4. **The K_P row cannot reject the old wiring.** `c1b_opac.py:191`.
   - Proposed tracking row: a copy with ibn ×1.05 (I_NC +8 %), requiring |ΔK_P/K_P| ≤ 2 %.
   - Expected: replica about 0 %, fixed wiring about −7.6 %, so it rejects with a margin of about 3.8×.
5. **Gate B corner coverage.** SPEC row 94 (B) lists tt/27, ss/−40, ss/125 and fs/125; gb.sh runs fs/125 only.
   - The worst phase margin is at ss/−40 boost (52.6°, fp2/GBW 1.11).
   - ss/125 boost span is 1.414 V, which needs the per-side swing check with V_OCM error.

## Minor
- Bank switch: put it in the gate path, not in series with the diode fingers. Ron of 5–20 kΩ in weak inversion gives about 3–12 % finger-current error.
- m_rt and m_rn sizes are typed in by hand. Derive them from m_t and m_n and test for equality (`test_c1_netlist.py:72-83` checks only node order).
- REF_TRIM is base-only.
- No Monte Carlo run of the replica or the ratios yet.
- Unexplained A0 gap of about 2.6 dB: WO-24 single-finger references gave 83.66 dB at fs/−40, D58 gives 80.43, and the D55 revert accounts for only about 0.6 dB. Cold m_pl/m_plc margins are 94–108 mV, on the soft-saturation cliff.
