### Minor

**H-m1. O0 is unconverged in either direction, not "optimistic by 2.8×".** PROVEN.

|---|---|---|
| O0 | `0.0 1.4 -6.7 -2.9 -2.3 -1.3 -0.2 … 1.9 1.7 -1.6 1.4 -1.9 0.0`. Jagged, no shape | −8.84e-5 |

- O0 hides about half of the physical incomplete-settling error, both the linear and the nonlinear part. It also adds a numerical floor of about ±2–7 ppm at N256.
- Mechanism (REASONED): gear/BDF2 damps oscillation numerically when the step is large. ω_n of the OTA1 loop on r1 is about 1.4e8 rad/s, so h·ω_n is about 1.4 at 10 ns but 0.27 at 1.9 ns.
- Consistent with this: trap at O1 gives 18.59 against 17.28 for gear at O1, and gear at O2 gives 18.77.
- Rows with high physical INL rise at O2: r1 ×2.8, A′(d) +34 %, T3 +24 %, OTA1 knee +0.3–0.4 V/µs.
- Rows with low physical INL fall: V4 −43 %, V4m −41 %, A′(c) −84 %, OTA2 N256 −13 to −25 %.
- This is not a model artefact. Reltol alone and tmax alone land on the same smooth curve (16.94 / 17.06), and trap agrees.
- Fix the wording in s5h §1 and §6.

**Q4.** The OTA2 interior optimum is plausible physics.
- Both OTAs reach their best INL at ζ ≈ 0.87–0.94 (PM 72–75°):
  - OTA1: ζ = 0.5·√(2.94/0.84) = 0.935;
  - OTA2: ζ = 0.5·√(1.58/0.497) = 0.89.
- Slightly underdamped settling of the post-slew linear transient is fastest. The S5g prediction of ζ = 1 was slightly off.
- OTA2's curve is flatter because its error is divided by the first-stage gain.
- Caveat (REASONED): the deck places the second pole before the tanh, whereas in a real folded cascode the fold pole comes after the pair. The steep low-mult side of the curve is therefore model-specific, and the D43a step row must stay the gate.
- There is no base-mode fp2 sweep yet. It is informational (0.25 against 1.333).
