# REVIEW S5g / WO-15: boosted operating points, solver sensitivity, fp2 sensitivity, gm/Id tooling (2026-11-09)

Reviewer: sd-adc-design-reviewer (read-only, ran nothing). The coordinator saved this text from the hand-back.

Scope: the uncommitted WO-15 working tree (`tier1_tb.py`, `gen_tier1.py`, `test_tier1.py`, `results/s5g_tier1.md`, `gmid_table.md`).

## Verdict
- **A′ closes REVIEW-S5f M2 as a point check.**
  - Cold A′ (30 MHz / 25 V/µs; 34 MHz / 27 V/µs): 0.880 ppm at N1024 (0.188 tight), 1.24 ppm at N256.
  - Hot A′: 1.079 / 1.375 ppm at N1024 and 2.01 / 2.00 ppm at N256.
  - Margin is ≥ 3.6× in every case. A GBW rise at the cold corner does not hurt INL in this deck.
- **fp2_mult 30 (101–113 ppm) is macro-model physics, not a deck bug.**
  - With fp2 = 3·GBW, OTA1 at β 0.84 is near critical damping (ζ ≈ 0.95). Its envelope settles about 1.8× faster than a single-pole OTA with the same GBW.
  - fp2_mult 30 removes that help.
- **SPEC gap.** SPEC asks for "PM ≥ 60° both". The fp2-held row sits at PM ≈ 63° and gives 177 ppm. The GBW-row evidence holds only at PM ≈ 75° (OTA1). The OTA2 row has no PM line.
- **Solver convergence is not shown.**
  - Only two option levels were tried.
  - The "tight" maxstep of 5 ns is about the closed-loop τ (≈ 5.3 ns at 4 MHz).
  - chgtol and trtol were not tightened.
- **The new min-W/L table is 2× too large**: it uses I_tail where the targets are per device.
- **S6 is not blocked. S7 is blocked** by G-M1, G-M2, G-M3 and the SPEC text.

## 1. Correctness

### Major

**G-M1. The deck's settling evidence needs PM ≈ 75° on OTA1. SPEC "PM ≥ 60°" admits a 177 ppm case and has no upper bound.** REASONED (hand calc on deck parameters; ppm values quoted).
- **Code:**
  - `gen_tier1.py:258–266`: second pole Rf 1 kΩ / Cf ahead of the tanh.
  - `params.py` `TIER1_FP2_MULT = 3`.
  - SPEC OTA1 row "PM ≥ 60° both"; OTA2 row has no PM line.
- **Closed loop** s²τ2 + s + K = 0, with K = β·2π·GBW.
  - **Tracking row (a)** (OTA1 20 MHz, fp2 60 MHz, β 0.84):
    - τ2·K = 0.28, ζ = 0.945;
    - envelope σ = 1.885e8 /s (τ 5.3 ns), against a single pole at K = 1.056e8 (9.5 ns);
    - over 50 ns the single pole leaves about 60× more error.
  - **s5f rows, fp2_mult 30** (OTA1 14.2 MHz):
    - dominant pole 7.7e7 against an envelope of 1.34e8 at fp2_mult 3;
    - about 30× more linear error, i.e. roughly 10 → 100 ppm, as observed;
    - T3 does not depend on N or GBW2, which fits OTA1 dominating.
  - **Held row (a)** (fp2 30 MHz, GBW 20 MHz):
    - τ2·K = 0.56, ζ = 0.67, PM ≈ 63°;
    - σ 9.4e7, half the tracking value: 177 ppm;
    - **that design passes the SPEC row.**
  - **OTA2** (β2 0.497, fp2 = 3·GBW):
    - ζ = 1.23, overdamped, PM ≈ 81°;
    - holding its pole at 45 MHz moves it towards critical damping, which is faster;
    - so the item-3 penalty is OTA1's.
- **Why it matters:**
  - The GBW rows were derived near OTA1's fastest-settling PM.
  - A folded cascode with its fold pole far out (PM 85°+) needs about 1.5–2× the GBW for the same settling.
  - PM 60–65° fails by about 50×.
- **Direction:**
  - Replace "PM ≥ 60°" with a PM window at the integration-phase β per OTA, in base and boost, at every corner:
    - OTA1 ≈ 70–80° (fp2/GBW ≈ 2.7–5);
    - OTA2 a window around fp2 ≈ 2–3·GBW (≈ 75–82°).
  - Preferred: a transistor-level closed-loop **step-settling row** (error ≤ X at t = h − 2g, worst step, real β and C_L).
  - Learning run: fp2_mult sweep 1.5/2/3/4/6/10/30 on OTA1 only at converged options. Expect a minimum near 4·β1 ≈ 3.4.

**G-M2. `gmid_min_wl` uses I_tail; the gm/Id targets are per device (I_tail/2). The table is 2× too large.** PROVEN.
- **Code:** `tier1_tb.py:1460` (`GMID_TARGETS` 35e-6 / 14.2e-6 / 18.9e-6), `:1504` `wl = id_a / jm`, `test_tier1.py:401–402` pins the convention.
- The target 10.05 = 2π·20 MHz·1.4 pF / 17.5 µA, and 17.5 µA is per device.
- Hand vs tool, L 1 µm:

  | Case | Hand (per device) | Tool |
  |---|---|---|
  | OTA1 | 29 | 56.3 |
  | OTA2 ×2 | 18 | 34.9 |
  | OTA2 ×1.5 | 106 | 237 |

- **Impact:** OTA1 W·L 56 µm² gives Cgs ≈ 0.25–0.3 pF per side, the whole Cp_vg1 budget (β1 0.84).
- **Direction:**
  - Use the per-device current.
  - Make the test non-vacuous against an independent hand value.
  - Regenerate the table.

**G-M3. Solver convergence is undemonstrated, and "tight" is itself coarse.** REASONED (mechanism) / PROVEN (shifts).
- **Code:** `tier1_tb.py:1718` (`tight` = reltol 1e-5, tmax 5 ns; chgtol 1e-14, trtol 7 and gear unchanged), `params.py:99`.
- Shifts between the two levels are large and of either sign: 6.70 → 18.61, 0.880 → 0.188, 1.079 → 1.375.
- **Time scales:**
  - At 4 MHz, τ_env ≈ 5.3 ns and the ringing period is ≈ 30 ns. tmax 10 ns is about 2τ, tmax 5 ns about τ.
  - Gear-2 damps the underdamped mode when h·ω_d ≈ 1, which is exactly the settling tail being measured.
  - At 2 MHz, τ_env ≈ 10.6 ns ≈ tmax.
- The T4 cubic-fit residual is 4.9–5.9 ppm at both N. A physical pattern-dependent residue would scale with N, so this points to solver noise at the size of the N1024 target.

### Minor
- **m1.** Control (a) proves the fast4 path reproduces s5f (3.686). It does not prove accuracy. Record the converged value as the SPEC evidence number.
- **m2.** `fp2_fixed` was held at the minimum base GBW. A realistic base (about 14 MHz) gives fp2 42 MHz, PM ≈ 70°, ζ ≈ 0.79. So 177 ppm is a worst case; the realistic penalty is probably tens of ppm. The conclusion "must track" stands.
- **m3.** The macro puts the second pole *ahead of* the tanh. In a folded cascode the fold-node pole sits after the pair's current limit, so real slew-to-linear recovery differs. Another reason to gate on a transistor step test.
- **m4.** SPEC "one switched bias leg (C6)" against the D41 default "fold/cascode switches with the tail". Both hold if one switched reference leg drives every mirror, but the text doesn't say so.
- **m5.** The L 0.35 µm rows are unchecked against A0 ≥ 80 dB and need the G-M2 fix first.

## 2. Efficiency
- **E1.** With G-M2 fixed, OTA2 ×2 needs W/L ≈ 17–18 (≈ 4 µm² at L 0.5, ≈ 17 µm² at L 1), Cgs ≈ 0.05–0.08 pF. That leaves ≈ 0.18 pF of the Cp_vg2 budget and confirms A′ is cheap.
- **E2.** An OTA2 PM window near critical damping (fp2 ≈ 2·GBW at β2 0.5) gives a ≈ 1.6× faster envelope, which could save a few µA of OTA2 boost. A learning step, not a v1 need.
- **E3.** Apply the full convergence ladder only to rows with < 3× margin. Everything else gets one tight confirmation.

**Convergence rule** (every gated tier-1 INL with < 3× margin, and every SPEC evidence number):
1. Ladder O0 → O1 → O2: each step divides reltol by 10, halves tmax, divides chgtol by 100; trtol 1 at O2.
2. Floor: tmax ≤ τ_cl/10 (≈ 0.5 ns at 4 MHz, ≈ 1 ns at 2 MHz).
3. Accept O2 if |INL(O2) − INL(O1)| ≤ max(10 %·INL, 0.1·(gate − INL)).
4. The verdict uses INL(O2)·(1 + |Δ|/INL).
5. One trap-vs-gear cross-check per mode.
6. Non-vacuity: show that O0 fails the rule on one row (the s5f 15 MHz N256 row already does).

**Minimal rerun set** (about 30 jobs, 2 at a time):

### Q2. fp2_mult 30
Physics of the two-pole macro (G-M1).
- The implementation is correct.
- Stiffness is not the issue: gear handles τ_f 0.37 ns.
- The signatures (independent of N and GBW2, ×10 size) fit physics, not numerics.
- Confirm with one T3 row at O2; expect about the same 100 ppm.

### Q3. SPEC rows that follow
1. OTA1: PM window ≈ 70–80° at β1, base and boost, every corner. Preferred: a closed-loop step-settling row at the real β, C_L and step, at t = h − 2g, for 2 MHz base and 4 MHz boost.
2. OTA2: add a PM or step row (window fp2/GBW ≈ 2–3 at β2 ≈ 0.5).
3. Bias: fold/cascode current scales with the tail in boost (I_fold/I_tail constant ±10 %, ≥ 1.2), as a SPEC row. Interpret "one switched bias leg" as one switched reference leg feeding every mirror.
4. Boost toggle at a pair boundary; bias, CMFB and fold settle within the reset cycles (C1 transient tb).
5. Check that the 172 µA A′ figure assumes the fold switches.
