## Fast mode
4 MHz needs ≈ 20–30 MHz loaded GBW for ≤ 8 ppm. Options:
- (a) keep 10 MHz and set a fast-mode INL target of ≥ 100 ppm;
- (b) 20 MHz minimum (≈ +46 µA);
- (c) a **bias-boost config bit** that doubles the OTA tails at 4 MHz.

Decide the D13 fast-mode target first. If it is ≥ 100 ppm, (a) is enough; if it is near precision, (c) beats (b).
