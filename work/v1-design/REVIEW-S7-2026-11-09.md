## 2. New Majors
- **S7-M1. The OTA step-settling gate has no threshold or g (SPEC l.94, l.96), so it cannot fail.**
  - Define h = 0.5/fs and g = 0.025/fs, so h − 2g = t_half (225 ns at 2 MHz, 112.5 ns at 4 MHz).
  - Gate on |ε(t_half)| ≤ ε_macro and on nonlinearity over 0.1–1.0 × max step ≤ ε_NL,macro. Take both numbers from the tier-1 macromodel step at the gated points (OTA1 12.5 V/µs / 10 MHz and the D39 boosted point; OTA2 13.5 / 10 MHz and 22.5 MHz / 27 V/µs). The worst OTA1 step is ≈ 0.49 V diff.
  - Hand estimate: ε ≈ 6e-5.
  - Stronger option: put the transistor OTA into the tier-1 deck.
- **S7-M2. Boost and trim are "latched at a pair boundary", but they are missing from §3.8, the §3.7a(d) latched set, the (e) invalid scope and the golden config.** No boost reset value is given; untrimmed fast mode is ≈ 265 µA. Add boost (1 b, reset 0) and trim (3 b, reset 100) to §3.8, §3.7a(d) and rule (10), plus golden v3 vectors.
- **S7-M3. The digital I/O is undefined:** no ui/uo/uio map, no config interface, no readout clock or buffering, no fs_div code table.
  - An 80-bit frame does not fit a 66-cycle N 64 slot at an fs-rate shift clock: it needs f_shift ≥ 80·fs/(N+2) = 4.85 MHz at 4 MHz / N 64.
  - Needs a §3.8a table: double-buffered frame register, external shift clock, drop shown as a frame_ctr gap.
- **S7-M4. §3.4 lists Cs2 1 pF / Cint2 2 pF, against §3.0 0.5 / 1 pF (C_side2 2.01 pF, β2 0.497), and Ck2 is missing.**
