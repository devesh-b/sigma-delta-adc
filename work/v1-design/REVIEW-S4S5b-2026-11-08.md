## Verdict

- **S5 (S5b): closable with conditions.** WO-08 closed M1, M2, m1–m6 properly. M3 and M4 were implemented, but each has one gap that changes how the results can be used:

What changes is the model term, which becomes the D12 residual ε_eff with a C4 allocation (see C-M3). This is not a relaxation: the 60 dB number becomes a C4 PEX+MC gate.

## 1. Correctness

### Critical
None.

### Major

**C-M1: the SR/GBW knees do not isolate OTA1, and the grid cannot resolve the margin.** PROVEN by code reading; the consequence is REASONED.
- **Where:** `design/tier1/gen_tier1.py`, v2 OTA loop `for k in (1, 2)`:
  ```
  gmk = bst * 6.283185307179586 * c["gbw"] * cl
  ip = bst * c["sr"] * cl
  ```
  Both OTAs take the same `c["sr"]` and `c["gbw"]`. Every `--knees2` run, including the spec point, therefore runs OTA2 at 12 V/µs, against SPEC §3.0's OTA2 SR ≥ 25 V/µs.
- **Why it matters:** an error injected into x2 enters the residue-reconstructed output as Σe2 / (c1·c2·N(N−1)/2) ≈ 2·ē2/(c1·c2·N).
  - At N 256 with c1·c2 = 0.0625, a mean signal-dependent slew error of 5e-4·VREF on x2 gives ≈ 60 ppm.
  - The x2 step c2·(x1 − k·y) is ≈ 1.1 V diff, roughly 2× the x1 step.

  So at N 256 the cliff between 8 and 10 V/µs may be partly or entirely OTA2's. OTA2 errors fall as 1/N and OTA1 errors do not, so the knee at N 4096 can differ.
- **Grid resolution:** 2 MHz values are 6/8/10/12/25; the knee lies between 8 (133 ppm) and 10 (3.08 ppm). At 4 MHz boost the values are 6/9/12/25, and 9 gives 265 ppm while 12 gives 6.99 ppm. "Zero margin at 4 MHz" is therefore a grid artefact: the true margin is somewhere between 0 and 33 %.
- **Fix direction:** sweep OTA1 SR and GBW with OTA2 held at its own spec (25 V/µs, 10 MHz), then sweep OTA2 with OTA1 at spec.
  - SR grid {8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13.5, 15} V/µs at 2 MHz;
  - boost base {9, 10, 11, 12, 13.5, 15} at 4 MHz;
  - N 256 and N 1024.

  That is about 40 runs. Decide on the SR row only after it.

**C-M2: the current estimate cannot deliver a symmetric 12 V/µs, and boost mode breaks the 200 µA cap (D31).** PROVEN by hand calculation; the topology assumption is the S3 folded cascode with diff SR = I_tail/C_L.
- **Where:** SPEC §3.0 "Current" row (OTA1 ≈ 46 µA, OTA2 ≈ 40 µA) and D31 (boost ≈ 198 µA).
- **Folded-cascode slew symmetry:** at full steering, one folding branch carries I_fold − I_tail. Symmetric ±I_tail/2 output slewing needs I_fold ≥ I_tail; use 1.2× for margin. Total OTA current ≈ I_tail + 2·I_fold ≈ 3.4·I_tail.
  - **46 µA total:** with I_fold ≥ I_tail, I_tail ≤ 15.3 µA, so the symmetric SR is at most 15.3 µA / 1.4 pF = **10.9 V/µs < 12**. With less fold current the slew becomes asymmetric, which is the 2815 ppm failure mode S5b found.
  - **OTA1:** 12 V/µs ⇒ I_tail 16.8 µA ⇒ **57 µA**; 15 V/µs ⇒ 21 µA ⇒ **71 µA**.
  - **OTA2:** 25 V/µs × 0.7 pF = 17.5 µA ⇒ **60 µA**, not 40.
- **"Spec holds at every corner":** bias current spread across R/temperature corners of about −20 % means the typical value must be ≈ 1.25× the worst-corner value.
- **Totals:** OTA1 at 12 V/µs worst-corner ⇒ ≈ 71 µA typical; OTA2 ≈ 60–75 µA; comparator 6; bias/CMFB 20.

  | Mode | Analog current | vs D31 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Precision (boost = 0) | ≈ 157–172 µA | inside 200 µA |
  | Boost (×2 on both OTAs) | ≈ 290–320 µA | breaks 200 µA |
  | Boost at the SPEC's own numbers, symmetric 12 V/µs, no corner factor | 57 + 60 = 117 µA extra ⇒ ≈ 260 µA | breaks 200 µA |
- **Fix direction:** a user decision on fast mode (options in §2 and §5). Precision mode fits.

**C-M3: the CMRR evidence leaves out the D12 residual mechanism, so sys-off ≥ 60 dB is not yet demonstrated.** Omission PROVEN by code reading; magnitude REASONED.
- **Where:** `gen_tier1.py`. `Cpv1/Cpv2` sit only on `vg1/vg2` (behind the integration switch). The floating top-plate nodes `tp1p/tp1n` carry only the `Rfl` 1e13 Ω bleeders, so they have no parasitic and no parasitic mismatch.
- **Mechanism.** Charge conservation on the shorted top node in phase 1, with Cp,top per side:

  V_t − V_g,cm = (Vin_cm − Vref_cm) · Cs/(Cs + Cp)

  so the differential charge contains

  (Vin_cm − Vref_cm) · [(ΔCs + ΔCp)·Cs/(Cs + Cp) − ΔCs]

  Input-referred:

  **ε_eff ≈ ΔCp,top/Cs − (Cp,top/Cs)·ε_Cs**, plus the charge-injection split term δ·(dq_inj/dV)/Cs of the shorting TG.
  - With Cp,top = 0 (the deck), ε_eff = 0 to first order. That is exactly what S5b sees.
  - The leakage-driven island-CM drift cancels between phases 1 and 2. Both V_t and V_g,cm shift together, so leakage is not a CMRR threat. The S5b caveat's "≈ 100 dB" leakage estimate is pessimistic. Leakage is a CM-range threat instead (C-M4).
- **Numbers.** Cs = 0.5 pF. Cp,top = TG diffusions of the shorting switch and the φ2 switch, plus capm fringe and routing: ≈ 10–20 fF.
  - (Cp/Cs)·ε_Cs = 0.04 × 0.75 % = 3e-4, i.e. 70 dB.
  - ΔCp,top of 0.5–2 fF (3–10 % layout asymmetry) gives 1e-3 to 4e-3, i.e. **60 to 48 dB**.
  - TG injection: dq/dV ≈ Cox·(Wn·Ln + Wp·Lp) ≈ 7 fF/V. The n and p slopes add, they do not cancel. With a 2 % split imbalance this gives 2.8e-4, i.e. 71 dB.
  - RSS at ΔCp = 0.25 fF: 5.6e-4, i.e. **65 dB**.

  60 dB is reachable, but only with ΔCp,top ≤ ~0.25 fF verified in PEX and the MIM capm plate on the floating node.
- **Sys on:** all of these terms are fixed in the modulator frame, so the pin-side swap cancels them. Sys-on ≥ 100 dB stays robust. What limits sys-on is the pin path in front of the swap: ΔR_pin × the CM pin current (m6).
- **Fix direction:**
  - Tier-1 `--cm` with Cp,top ∈ {10, 20} fF per side and ΔCp ∈ {0, 0.25, 1} fF. Expected: the ε_eff closed form within 25 %. Broken copy: ΔCp 2 fF must fail 60 dB.
  - The S4 model takes ε_eff, not ε_Cs (see §5).
  - The real number is a C4 (PEX+MC) and C10 (layout symmetry) gate.

**C-M4: the input-pair CM range (proposed 1.45–1.85 V) is likely infeasible at ss/−40/3.0 V, and it omits D12 island drift.** REASONED; the PDK V_th must be checked.
- **Headroom.** The island CM is set at reset to the output CM, `CM = VAPWR/2` = 1.65 V (params). A 3.3 V-capable PMOS is the thick-oxide pfet_g5v0d10v5. Paul Thomas's notes put thick-oxide V_th at "~0.8 V" at tt, and it rises at ss/−40.
  - At 1.85 V + V_SG ≈ 1.1 V (ss/−40, weak inversion), the source sits at ≈ 2.95 V, leaving **≈ 0.05 V** of tail headroom at VAPWR 3.0 V.
  - At 1.65 V it is ≈ 0.25 V: no cascoded tail is possible.
  - Paul moved VCM to 0.9 V for exactly this reason (`repos/sd-analog/pthomas-sigma-delta-design/DESIGN.md`, lines 205–216 and 305–312).
- **D12 drift.** With D12 the island {vg±, tops, Cint plates} has no CM refresh during a conversion. The no-D12 topology is refreshed every cycle through the tops tied to cm. Drift = I_leak · N/fs / C_island, with C_island ≈ 2×(0.5 + 4 + 0.26) pF ≈ 9.5 pF.
  - 100 pA (SPEC leakage row, ff/125/3.6) at 2 MHz, N 4096: **21 mV**.
  - At 0.25 MHz, N 4096 (16 ms): **≈ 170 mV**.
  - The deck's 1e12/1e13 Ω bleeders anchor the island and hide this.
- **Fix direction:**
  - State the range relative to the island CM, V_icm.
  - Pick V_icm in C1 for ≥ 0.2 V tail headroom at ss/−40/3.0; probably ≈ 1.1–1.3 V. Either set the CMFB reference there or reset the island to a separate V_icm.
  - Add the drift term to the row.
  - Add a tier-1 island-leakage case.

### Minor

**m1: the CMRR metric is mis-defined.** PROVEN from the JSON.
- **Sys off.** S5b computes (off(mm, Vcm) − off(0, Vcm)) / (Vcm − 1.024). That is the mismatch-induced offset at one CM, not dVoff/dVin_cm. Strict slope with D12 at 0.75 %: off_V = 2.119e-7 / 2.148e-7 / 2.186e-7 V at Vin_cm 0.2 / 1.0 / 2.0, i.e. 6.7 nV over 1.8 V, which is **≈ 168 dB** (reported: 132 dB).
- **Sys on.** It uses `pair_V` = (e0 − e1)/2. That is the *gain* term: −1.097e-5 V = gain error × u0 0.08 × VREF in every run. "Sys-on CMRR 140 dB" is therefore a mismatch-induced gain change, not a CM error.
- **Effect:** both definitions still meet spec here. They would mislead at C4, where a matched-run CM dependence (Cp,top) gets subtracted away.
- **Fix:** define CMRR as the slope across Vin_cm at fixed mismatch, for the plain offset (sys off) and for the pair-demodulated offset (sys on).

**m2: the CMRR gate does not discriminate.** PROVEN from the results.
- No broken copy fails `d12_rejects_cm`: no-D12 passes at 66 dB.
- The only discriminator is the vg-CM range check, and it uses the ASSUMED `TIER1_VG_CM_RANGE = (0.2, 2.0)`. Under that range no-D12 at Vin_cm 2.0 stays in range.
- **Fix:** the ΔCp broken copy from C-M3, and the real range from C-M4.

**m3: the xcheck A0 80 row sits on the null floor.** REASONED.
- Deck INL 0.413 vs model 0.295 ppm, 33 % against a 35 % limit. The REVIEW-S5 probe gave a deck value of 0.29.
- The deck default Roff of 1e12 gives a 4.6e-7 floor (0.46 ppm), which is the same size as the quantity compared.
- **Fix:** rerun the row with Roff 1e14.

**m4: deck INL is systematically 17–33 % above the model at constant A0.** PROVEN from the results.
- Same sign in all four cases; unexplained. The β/Cp treatment differs between the deck (β 0.84) and the model.
- The model-based S4/S7 INL gates should carry a ×1.4 factor, or the cause should be found.
- With A(v) the sign reverses (deck 3.18 vs model 3.65).

**m5: INL contributions do not add.** PROVEN from the results.
- 2 MHz spec point: 1.69 ppm. Spec + A(v): 1.01 ppm. A(v) alone, ideal dynamics: 3.18 ppm.
- Settling and gain compression partly cancel. That cancellation is not guaranteed at corners.
- **Fix:** gate each contributor separately, or take the worst of {separate, combined}.

**m6: the SPEC input-source row uses the no-D12 pin-current formula.** REASONED.
- With D12, the CM part of the pin charge per cycle is Cs·Cp/(Cs + Cp)·(Vin_cm − Vref_cm) ≈ Cp,top·ΔVcm. That is **≈ 30 nA**, not 1 µA, at 2 MHz, 15 fF and 1 V.
- Sys-on CMRR from the pin path is ≈ 1/(ΔR·fs·Cp,top). ΔR 50 Ω (10 % of the 500 Ω `tt_asw_3v3` path) gives ≈ 116 dB, so the 120 dB goal is limited here.
- **Fix:** an `--rsrc` run with a Vin_cm sweep and ΔR.

**m7: text error in S5b §6 finding 2.** "D12 numbers are floor-limited (bleeders)" is contradicted by the data.
- The mismatch offset scales as ε²: (3.55 − 1.35)e-8 = 2.2e-8 at 0.25 %, and 1.98e-7 at 0.75 %. The ratio is 9.0 = 3².
- It is a second-order term. A floor would not scale, and the bleeder term would be first order.

**m8: S4 n15 margin is 0.7 %.** 2.98 vs 3.0 µV at ss/125/3, 0.25 MHz.
- It passes, and the model's OTA excess (2.05) is pessimistic per S3-1 (≈ 1.17), so the real margin is larger.
- Report the margin column; don't treat this as a closure.

**m9: `--knees2` ACCEPT is run-health only.** Spec FLAGs (4 MHz no-boost 6252 ppm, asymmetric SR 2815 ppm) do not change the exit code. That is fine per the WO, but label the row "run-health ACCEPT".

## 2. Efficiency

- **Boost only what limits.** If C-M1 shows OTA2 is not the 4 MHz limiter, boost OTA1 alone. That saves ≈ 60 µA in boost mode, which is the difference between ≈ 260 µA and ≈ 200 µA.
- **Keep C_L1 at 1.4 pF.** SC-CMFB caps ≤ 50 fF, Cs2 load only in the hold phase (D25). Each +0.1 pF on C_L1 costs ≈ 7 % SR, i.e. ≈ 4–5 µA of OTA1 current at constant SR.
- **Drop the Cs role-swap (dem) variant from the design space.** It gives no sys-off gain and worsens sys-on to 106–126 dB. That saves 8 switches and the clock gating.
- **CMRR ownership.** With D12, sys-off CMRR is a layout-symmetry problem (ΔCp,top), not a cap-area problem. No need to grow Cs for matching: the 27 pF/cap figure in D32 is moot.
- **Lower V_icm (C-M4).** It frees ≈ 0.4 V of PMOS tail headroom. That allows a cascoded tail and better PSRR/CMRR of the OTA itself at no current cost.

## 4. What held up

- **D12 first-order CM cancellation is real** in the ideal deck.
  - Mismatch offset is Vin_cm-independent: slope 3.7 nV/V, i.e. 168 dB.
  - It scales as ε², so it is second order.
  - No-D12 behaves as first order: 75.9 → 66.3 dB for 3× ε, which is 20·log 3. The settling closed form gives 7.2 % predicted vs 6.4 % measured.
- **The S4 model's ε_Cs·ΔVcm term** is the right hypothesis test for the sys-chop algebra. Its sys-on cancellation is confirmed in SPICE (no-D12: 66 → 134 dB). It is the wrong mechanism only for the D12 sys-off number.
- **Offsets:**
  - Vos2 = 2·Vos2·(1 + Ck/Cs2)/(c1(N−1)): measured 851.5 vs 847.1 µV. The 1.5× factor is correct because Ck shares OTA2's input node.
  - Vos1 path 9564.72 vs 9564.71 µV.
  - OTA chop Vos/(N−1) to 0.01 %.
  - The order-1 identity is exact.
  - Late-swap broken copy: −4800 µV.
- **Static A(v):** deck vs model 13.9 %; broken copy 536 ppm.
- **Noise transfer:** ratio 1.05, slope −1.043, correlation −1.000.
- **Gain error ≈ −1.09·k/A0**, independent of c1; text fixed.
- **Null-floor cause fixed**; SPICE_OPTIONS_TIER1 scope and requalification rule written.
- **D12 makes the vg CM inside a conversion Vin_cm-independent:** 1.630–1.728 V. CMFB settles to 1.8 mV at 0.2·t_half.
- **4 MHz without boost fails** (6252 ppm), so the boost bit is needed. **Asymmetric slew fails**, so the worst-direction rule is needed.
- **R_src 1 kΩ:** 0.00 ppm, so the SPEC row holds.

## 5. Recommendations on the questions asked

**CMRR reconciliation (S4 sys-off line).**
- Recommended: keep the gate at 60 dB and model the *right* term.
  - In `s4_corners`, replace ε_Cs with ε_eff = ΔCp,top/Cs + (Cp,top/Cs)·ε_Cs (+ injection allocation) at the C4 allocation: Cp,top 20 fF/side; ΔCp,top MC σ ≈ 0.08 fF (3σ 0.25 fF); ε_Cs from the MC draws.
  - That gives ≈ 65 dB sys-off; the gate passes with a thin margin that is stated as such.
  - Log the change in DECISIONS: it is a mechanism correction, not a relaxation, and the 60 dB number moves to C4 as a PEX+MC gate.
- Not recommended:
  - modelling D12 in `iadc`: it can only reproduce the ideal cancellation;
  - carrying the 132–152 dB SPICE figure as evidence: the deck has Cp,top = 0 and the metric is wrong (m1).
- Realistic C4 target:
  - sys off ≥ 60 dB minimum, **66 dB design target** (ε_eff ≤ 5e-4) at PEX + MC 3σ;
  - sys on ≥ 100 dB minimum, 120 dB goal, bounded by ΔR_pin·fs·Cp,top (≈ 116 dB at 50 Ω).

**SR spec.** Options (a), (b) and (c) as posed are each incomplete.
- Recommended: **SR ≥ 12 V/µs diff in the slower direction, at every §6 corner, at loaded C_L**, with a margin rule: SR_min ≥ 1.25 × knee(OTA1 alone, at the precision N), knee resolved to ≤ 10 %.
  - Add the design rule I_fold ≥ 1.2·I_tail at every corner.
  - At 2 MHz this has 1.25× margin if the OTA1-only knee is ≤ 9.6 V/µs. The current confounded knee sits between 8 and 10, so C-M1 must confirm it.
- In practice, "12 at the worst corner" means ≈ 15 typical. That is option (a)'s cost: OTA1 ≈ 71 µA, precision total ≈ 160–170 µA, inside 200 µA.
- Fast mode cannot meet both ×2 boost on both OTAs and ≤ 200 µA (C-M2). Option (c), more than 2× boost, makes it worse. The choices for the user:
  - (i) boost OTA1 only, if C-M1 allows it;
  - (ii) keep 4 MHz targets as "reported, gated at N 1024 only";
  - (iii) fast fs 3 MHz;
  - (iv) lift the 200 µA cap for boost mode only.
