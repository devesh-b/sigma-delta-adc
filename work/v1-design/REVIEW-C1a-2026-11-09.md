# REVIEW C1a: OTA1 + CMFB design note (WO-20), 2026-11-09

Reviewer: sd-adc-design-reviewer. Read-only: no simulations, only the run3 op dump, the code and PDK greps. Condensed by the coordinator.

## Verdict
**REVISE NOTE FIRST** (rev B plus a re-spot with a fixed script at 45 PVT × 2 modes). C1b tooling may start in parallel. The topology is right; there is no Critical finding.

## Major
- **M1. A0 failure is headroom, PROVEN.**
  - At ss/125 boost, m_pl is in triode (Vsd 0.453 < Vdsat 0.456) and m_n sits at the edge (0.11 V margin). Hand A0 is 57.2 dB against SIM 58.3.
  - Longer L at the same W raised Vdsat ×1.4, which is why it did not help.
  - The V_SDL/V_FOLD bias is fixed at 0.45 V while Vdsat spans 0.15–0.46 V.
  - ff/125 base (75.3 dB) is a separate cause: intrinsic gain loss.
  - Fix:
    - m_pl W ≈ 100–120 µm at L 4, m_n W ≈ 56–80 µm;
    - Vdsat-tracking cascode bias;
    - cascodes at L ≈ 2 with W ×2.
  - Stack check: 0.75 ≤ 0.80 V.
  - Hand result after the fix: base ≈ 80 dB at ff/125 (marginal), boost hot ≈ 69–75 dB. A plain FC cannot reach 80 dB in boost; see P1. No gain boosters.
- **M2. fp2/GBW of 10–12 puts the fast 5 ppm gate at risk.**
  - The high-mult side of S5h (×10 → 21, ×30 → 28.5 ppm) does not depend on pole order, so it transfers to the real OTA. At 21.8 MHz hot, expect ≈ 10–15 ppm.
  - The ×3 optimum window is narrow (×2 → 10.9, ×4 → 8.06 ppm). Spread in gm_nc, fold C, MIM and other poles will not hold it.
  - Next:
    - (a) single-pole INL vs GBW 20–30 MHz (hand estimate: ≈ 24–28 MHz needed);
    - (b) a pole-after-tanh macro at mult 2/3/4/10;
    - then gate (B) at the hot corners.
  - The robust route is more gm1 (a wider pair). The fold cap is only a fine-tune.
- **M3. The protection-cascode bias cannot work as built.**
  - V_DIN 1.25 V still gives V_PC −0.03 V; the note added 0.15 V twice.
  - The lower-CM limit used |Vt| 0.45 V, but the measured Vsg is 0.86–1.24 V. At 1.25 V the actual margin is ≈ 0.3 V.
  - Fix: tie the m_pp gate to a fixed low voltage and let V_DIN float (0.80–1.29 V). Stress then stays ≤ 1.45 V, and one bias leg goes away.
- **M4. The tail clamp never conducts** (it needs tail > 3.7 V). The "gates high" fault is reachable at power-up through Cint coupling (≈ 2.5 V).
  - Fix: bound vg by construction, with the reset and island switches closed by default whenever rst_n is low or the bias is invalid.
  - The broken copy becomes: reset default open at power-up ⇒ REJECT.
- **M5. The input chopper `nfet_01v8` at vg CM 1.07–1.33 V with 1.62 V drive is about off.** It sits in series with the gates even when parked, so the OTA is blocked.
  - Fix: a 3.3 V device or TG with level-shifted clocks (C8). Redo the injection and Cp_vg numbers.
  - The fold-node chopper is fine.
- **M6. Gate (B) corners miss the binding hot corner.** The lowest GBW is at ss/125 and ff/125. Add ss/125/3.0 and fs/125/3.0 in boost (P5).
- **M7. The spot script is not evidence.**
  - A(v) uses the target Vt, not the measured V(op,om). A lost servo prints a number instead of REJECT.
  - ss/125 base is a **dead OTA** (|H| < 1), not a tooling glitch.
  - The servo-cap noise hypothesis is falsified by hand. Suspect a device noise model or bin.
  - The MC `repeat/op/reset` loop likely does not redraw agauss, so it is a fake MC.
- **M8. Noise mutants.**
  - "Sinks W÷4" lowers the noise; it points the wrong way.
  - 4 kΩ adds only 8 %, so it passes.
  - Use C_L ÷ 6 (≈ 318 µV ⇒ REJECT) and a series gate R ≈ 65 kΩ, and keep the ±30 % expected-value check.

## Minor
- **m1.** Itemise C_L: Cc going 0.1 → 0.2 pF alone drops SR to 12.3 V/µs.
- **m2.** CMFB section: range check, its ro costs ≈ −1.5 dB, and the Cr phase must differ from the Cs2 sample phase.
- **m3.** Boost-toggle settling within 500 ns (m_pl gate Cgg ≈ 1 pF).
- **m4.** Keep I_sink/I_tail at 1.35 and drop option (c).
- **m5.** Add the sf/fs corners; fs/125 is co-worst.
- **m6.** The 3.3 V device has vth0_slope 0 but mismatch comes in through toxe/voff/nfactor. Measure it with a mirror-pair MC.
- **m7.** List Vdsat per device per corner in the swing table.
